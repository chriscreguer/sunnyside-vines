Featured Producer · Ficha de Cata

304 Días en Barrica

Ribera del DueroDenominación de Origen

Bodega Peñafalcón is a family winery in Peñafiel, the heart of Spain's Ribera del Duero. Founded by Casimiro Marcos and María José Arranz, the estate draws its Tempranillo from its own pagos of limestone soil, ferments with native yeasts, and rests the wine in American and French oak — bold, expressive reds that balance power with finesse.

Nota de Cata — 2021, 10 meses Medium-deep cherry red, bright and clean. Ripe plum, blackberry and dark cherry lift over a subtle roast of vanilla, sweet spice and black liquorice; soft, mature tannins carry a long and generous finish.

Our Market · New York

We bring authentic, estate-grown wines to New York.

Based in the greater New York area, we serve a vibrant community of wine lovers across the city, Nassau County and upstate. Our proximity to one of the world's most dynamic food-and-wine markets lets us bring distinctive European wines directly to the restaurants, shops and tables that value authenticity and craftsmanship — connecting local retailers and sommeliers with small, family-run producers whose wines reflect a true sense of place.
