Bodega Peñafalcón · Ribera del Duero, Spain

Gran Reserva 2007

A complex, full-bodied Tempranillo from Ribera del Duero. Deep cherry-red with subtle mahogany hints; stewed red and black fruits meet roasted coffee and toasted wood. Hand-picked grapes are selected in the vineyard and winery, then aged for sixty months in French and American oak. The palate is velvety and rich, with mature tannins. Decant for 30–60 minutes before serving.

Grape
100% Tempranillo (Tinto Fino)
Alcohol
15% vol
Aging
60 months in French and American oak
Serve
16–18 °C; decant 30–60 minutes
Pairs with
Ribeye, rich stews, roast lamb and aged cheeses
