Convento Oreja · Ribera del Duero, Spain

Roble 2025

Tempranillo

Bright cherry with violet tones. Intense fresh fruit on the nose — strawberry and raspberry mingled with hints of spice and a slight balsamic fragrance. Fresh, full and fruity on the palate; well balanced, easy to drink, with a long finish. Raised three months in French and American oak from vines fifteen to twenty-five years old.

Serve
14–16 °C, lightly cooled
Pairs with
Pizza, burgers, midweek tapas
