Convento Oreja · Ribera del Duero, Spain

Roble 2025

Tempranillo fermented in temperature-controlled stainless steel and aged for three months in equal parts French and American oak. Cherry-red, clean and crystalline. Blueberry, raspberry and blackberry meet pastry aromas, light spice and balsamic notes. Soft, rounded tannins bring a broad, velvety texture, balanced acidity and a persistent finish.

Grape
100% Tempranillo (Tinto del País)
Alcohol
14% vol
Aging
3 months in French and American oak (50% each)
Pairs with
Grilled red meat, roasted lamb, portobello mushrooms and Manchego
