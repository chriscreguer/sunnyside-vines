Cooper Cellars – Los Ángeles · Campo de Borja, Spain

Las Suertes 2024

A natural orange wine made from Moscatel de Alejandría grown above 700 metres. Fermented in stainless steel with one month on the skins, then matured for twelve months in 300-litre oak barrels. Amber in color, with intense lychee and quince aromas. Dry, crisp and refreshing, with hints of honey, apricot and bitter orange marmalade. Limited production of 4,000 bottles.
