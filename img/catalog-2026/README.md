Catalog naming is retained. The updated catalog replaces Selección de Familia 2020 with Memoria Reserva 2022; the site now has a dedicated product entry and a redirect from the former URL. For Peñafalcón 14 Años, 2003 follows the product page and bottle label (the catalog index says 2023). Los Ángeles names follow the catalog index sequence and existing site product categories; no vintage was added to those filenames.

All ten images are used on the homepage, shop, and product pages. Individual catalog pages are available as product spec sheets in `docs/catalog-2026/`. Existing demo prices are retained because the catalog supplies no prices. Romeroso’s existing 2021 vintage is retained because the catalog does not specify a replacement vintage.
